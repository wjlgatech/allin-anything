One front door to the digital & physical world

What it does today. Imagine a school where every club is genuinely great, but nobody knows which door to knock on. allin-anything is the front desk. You say what you want — "design a phone stand I can 3D-print," "check my budget," "turn my sketch into a floor plan" — and a router (a real program, not vibes) names the right tool and shows how much of that tool has been machine-verified: white = just listed, yellow = facts pinned to a commit, green = its own tests ran here and passed. Nothing gets a badge without proof. It already runs whole chains — hand-drawn ink became a floor plan that passed five building checks — and it can run some chains by itself, but it obeys a security exam it deliberately failed: it always stops and hands the risky step to a human.

After the roadmap. Every tool green. More chains, including learn-anything-with-animations. Autonomy raised only when the security blockers actually close — never before. A weekly robot check flags anything stale. One front desk where every promise carries its receipt.
